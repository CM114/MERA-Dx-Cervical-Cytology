# Limitations and reporting boundaries

- The main XUData TBS5 results are development-stage image/crop-level results.
- The current record does not support patient-level or slide-level claims.
- No external confirmation cohort is represented by the public aggregate files.
- The risk layer is exploratory and must not be described as a clinically validated decision-support system.
- Raw images, model weights, and complete per-image predictions are outside the public repository.
- Third-party baseline implementations remain subject to their original licenses and may require separate acquisition.
- A GitHub repository alone is not a persistent data archive or a dataset license.
